Problem Statements

Select exactly 2 questions below, then click "Save Selection to Sheet" to confirm.

Create two classes: Student and Result.

Student class stores: Student name, Roll number, Marks of 3 subjects.

Result class calculates & displays: Total marks, Percentage, and Pass/Fail (Pass only if ≥ 40 marks in each subject).

Create two classes: Customer and ElectricityBill.

Customer class stores: Customer name, Customer ID, Units consumed.

Units Consumed Rate
0 – 100 ₹2 / unit
101 – 200 ₹3 / unit
201 – 300 ₹5 / unit
Above 300 ₹7 / unit

Display customer's name, units consumed, and total bill.

Create two classes: Employee and Salary.

Take Employee name, Employee ID, Basic salary. Calculate:

  • HRA = 20% of basic salary
  • DA = 10% of basic salary
  • Gross Salary = Basic + HRA + DA

If Gross > ₹50,000 → "High Salary", else → "Normal Salary".

Create two classes: Account and ATM.

Take account holder name, account number, account balance, and withdrawal amount. Check:

  • Amount > balance → "Insufficient Balance"
  • Amount not multiple of ₹100 → "Enter amount in multiples of 100"
  • Otherwise → perform withdrawal and display remaining balance

Create two classes: Customer and Shopping.

Take customer name and total shopping amount. Apply discount:

Shopping Amount Discount
Below ₹5,000 No discount
₹5,000 – ₹9,999 10%
₹10,000 – ₹19,999 15%
₹20,000 or above 20%

Display Customer name, Shopping amount, Discount, and Final amount.

Create two classes: Employee and Attendance.

Take Name, ID, Total working days, and Days present. Calculate attendance percentage:

  • 80% or above → "Good Attendance"
  • 60% to below 80% → "Average Attendance"
  • Below 60% → "Poor Attendance"

Create two classes: Customer and Restaurant.

Use switch to select: 1. Pizza (₹250), 2. Burger (₹150), 3. Sandwich (₹100), 4. Coffee (₹80).

Take item and quantity. If bill > ₹1,000, apply a 10% discount.

Create two classes: Consumer and Consumption.

Take Name, Consumer number, Previous and Current meter reading. Calculate units:

  • Below 100 units → "Low Consumption"
  • 100–300 units → "Normal Consumption"
  • Above 300 units → "High Consumption"

Display total units and consumption tier.

Create two classes: Customer and Loan.

Take Name, Age, Monthly salary, and Credit score. Eligible if:

  • Age ≥ 21
  • Monthly salary ≥ ₹25,000
  • Credit score ≥ 700

Display "Loan Eligible" or "Loan Not Eligible".

Create two classes: Customer and MovieTicket.

Take Name, Number of tickets, and Ticket price. Apply discounts:

  • 1–2 tickets → No discount
  • 3–5 tickets → 10% discount
  • More than 5 tickets → 15% discount

Display final bill.

Create two classes: Vehicle and Parking.

Take Vehicle number, Type, and Hours parked. Switch options:

  • 1. Bike → ₹20/hour
  • 2. Car → ₹40/hour
  • 3. Bus → ₹60/hour

If parked for > 5 hours, add an extra ₹50 parking charge.

Create two classes: Customer and Recharge.

Switch plan selector:

Plan Validity
₹199 28 days
₹299 30 days
₹399 56 days
₹499 84 days

Display Customer name, Selected plan, Amount, and Validity.
